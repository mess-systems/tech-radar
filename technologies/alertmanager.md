## What it is

Alert routing and deduplication for Prometheus (Apache-2.0). Official site: [prometheus.io/docs/alerting/latest/alertmanager](https://prometheus.io/docs/alerting/latest/alertmanager/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries the `alerts` capability.

## Alternatives considered

Grafana alerting. Chosen as the standard companion to Prometheus.
