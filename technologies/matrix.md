## What it is

Open standard for decentralised, end-to-end encrypted messaging. Official site: [matrix.org](https://matrix.org/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries the `chat` capability as the default.

## Alternatives considered

Mattermost (Trial). Chosen for an open, federated protocol.
