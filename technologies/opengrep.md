## What it is

Open-source static analysis engine, a community fork of Semgrep CE (LGPL-2.1). Official site: [opengrep.dev](https://www.opengrep.dev/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Security rules on code in CI.

## Alternatives considered

Semgrep CE. Chosen for an open license on the full engine.

## Where it applies

- [Capability codes: `scan`](https://github.com/mess-systems/naming-standard/blob/main/01-naming-conventions.md#43-capability-codes-unique-across-all-domains)

## Review trigger

A license or maintenance change upstream, or a better fit for the capability.
