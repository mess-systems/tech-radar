## What it is

Open-source source of truth for networks and infrastructure (Apache-2.0). Official site: [netboxlabs.com/oss/netbox](https://netboxlabs.com/oss/netbox/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Inventory of networks and hardware.

## Alternatives considered

Spreadsheets, a CMDB in ITSM. Trial: limited scope.
