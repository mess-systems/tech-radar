## What it is

Open-source high-throughput LLM inference server (Apache-2.0). Official site: [vllm.ai](https://vllm.ai/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries GPU inference behind agentgateway.

## Alternatives considered

llama.cpp (Assess), SGLang. Chosen for batching throughput.

## Where it applies

- [Capability codes: `inference`](https://github.com/mess-systems/naming-standard/blob/main/01-naming-conventions.md#43-capability-codes-unique-across-all-domains)
