## What it is

Open-source feature flag management platform (Apache-2.0 core). Official site: [getunleash.io](https://www.getunleash.io/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries the `flags` capability: releases are separated from deploys.

## Alternatives considered

Flagsmith, OpenFeature-only setups. Chosen for maturity and self-hosting.

## Where it applies

- [Capability codes: `flags`](https://github.com/mess-systems/naming-standard/blob/main/01-naming-conventions.md#43-capability-codes-unique-across-all-domains)

## Review trigger

A license or maintenance change upstream, or a better fit for the capability.
