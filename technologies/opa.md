## What it is

General-purpose policy engine with the Rego language (Apache-2.0, CNCF graduated). Official site: [openpolicyagent.org](https://www.openpolicyagent.org/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries the `policy` capability as policy as code.

## Alternatives considered

Kyverno for Kubernetes only. Chosen for one engine across systems.
