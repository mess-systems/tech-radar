## What it is

Open-source wiki engine (AGPL-3.0) with Markdown editing and git-backed storage. Official site: [js.wiki](https://js.wiki/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

It is the example for the `wiki` capability in the worked example (`wiki.collab.corp`).

## Alternatives considered

Other wiki engines. Trial because the standard names it as an illustration.

## Where it applies

- [Services: intake and encoding](https://github.com/mess-systems/naming-standard/blob/main/02-worked-example.md#3-services-intake-and-encoding)

## Review trigger

After it has carried the `wiki` capability in a limited scope: promote to Adopt or move to Hold.
