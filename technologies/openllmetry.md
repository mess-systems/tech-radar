## What it is

Open-source OpenTelemetry instrumentation for LLM apps (Apache-2.0). Official site: [github.com/traceloop/openllmetry](https://github.com/traceloop/openllmetry).

## Why mess.systems uses it

LLM traces in OpenTelemetry form, sent to Langfuse and Tempo.

## Alternatives considered

Vendor SDKs. Chosen to keep LLM traces in OpenTelemetry.
