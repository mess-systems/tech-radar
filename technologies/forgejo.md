## What it is

Community-governed self-hosted git forge (GPL-3.0) with built-in Actions CI, package registry and wiki. Official site: [forgejo.org](https://forgejo.org/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries the `git` capability, with Forgejo Actions for CI and the built-in wiki for project docs; replaces Gitea.

## Alternatives considered

Gitea, GitLab CE. Chosen for community governance and a free license with CI and wiki in one product.
