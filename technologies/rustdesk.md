## What it is

Open-source remote desktop with a self-hosted server (AGPL-3.0). Official site: [rustdesk.com](https://rustdesk.com/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Remote support for devices without third-party relays.

## Alternatives considered

TeamViewer, AnyDesk. Chosen for self-hosting.
