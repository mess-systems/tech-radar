## What it is

Multi-model database (documents, graph, key/value) with its own query language; BSL-1.1 core. Official site: [surrealdb.com](https://surrealdb.com/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Considered for graph-shaped data.

## Alternatives considered

PostgreSQL (Adopt), CouchDB (Adopt). Assess: license and maturity.
