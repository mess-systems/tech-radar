## What it is

Open-source metadata platform and data catalog (Apache-2.0). Official site: [open-metadata.org](https://open-metadata.org/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries the `data-catalog` capability; replaces DataHub.

## Alternatives considered

DataHub. Chosen for simpler operation.

## Where it applies

- [Capability codes: `data-catalog`](https://github.com/mess-systems/naming-standard/blob/main/01-naming-conventions.md#43-capability-codes-unique-across-all-domains)
