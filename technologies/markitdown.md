## What it is

Open-source converter of documents to Markdown for LLMs (MIT). Official site: [github.com/microsoft/markitdown](https://github.com/microsoft/markitdown).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries the `convert` capability for documents fed to models.

## Alternatives considered

Docling, Pandoc. Chosen for broad formats and simplicity.

## Where it applies

- [Capability codes: `convert`](https://github.com/mess-systems/naming-standard/blob/main/01-naming-conventions.md#43-capability-codes-unique-across-all-domains)

## Review trigger

A license or maintenance change upstream, or a better fit for the capability.
