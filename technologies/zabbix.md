## What it is

Open-source monitoring for networks, servers and devices (AGPL-3.0). Official site: [zabbix.com](https://www.zabbix.com/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Considered for network and device monitoring where Prometheus exporters are weak.

## Alternatives considered

Prometheus with SNMP exporters (Adopt). Assess: overlap with the Prometheus stack.
