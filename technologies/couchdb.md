## What it is

Open-source document database with replication (Apache-2.0). Official site: [couchdb.apache.org](https://couchdb.apache.org/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries the `docstore` capability; replaces MongoDB.

## Alternatives considered

MongoDB (SSPL). Chosen for an open license and sync.
