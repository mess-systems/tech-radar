## What it is

Container-based streaming of browsers and desktops. Official site: [kasmweb.com](https://kasmweb.com/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Isolated browsers and desktops for risky or shared work.

## Alternatives considered

Plain VDI. Chosen for container isolation.
