## What it is

Open-source durable execution platform for long-running workflows (MIT). Official site: [temporal.io](https://temporal.io/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries workflow orchestration in code; replaces Kestra and is the base for own ITSM.

## Alternatives considered

Kestra, Airflow for data only. Chosen for durable workflows in code.
