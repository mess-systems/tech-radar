## What it is

Open-source realtime audio and video platform with an agents framework (Apache-2.0). Official site: [livekit.io](https://livekit.io/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Voice and video for agents.

## Alternatives considered

Plain WebRTC. Chosen for the agents framework.
