## What it is

Open-source identity management for Linux: LDAP directory, Kerberos, host-based access and an internal CA. Official site: [freeipa.org](https://www.freeipa.org/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Considered for the `directory` capability for Linux hosts.

## Alternatives considered

Authentik's built-in directory, Samba AD. Assess: decide whether a separate Linux directory is needed.
