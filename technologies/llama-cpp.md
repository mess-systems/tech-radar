## What it is

Open-source LLM inference in C/C++ (MIT) for CPUs, GPUs and Apple silicon, with an OpenAI-compatible server. Official site: [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Considered for small and edge inference behind agentgateway.

## Alternatives considered

vLLM (Adopt) for GPU serving. Assess: fit for heterogeneous and low-resource nodes.
