## What it is

Lightweight multi-format artifact registry and proxy cache (MIT), single binary. Official site: [getnora.dev](https://getnora.dev/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Carries the `hub-cache` and `packages` capability for package formats.

## Alternatives considered

Nexus, Artifactory. Chosen for a small footprint; Harbor stays for OCI.

## Where it applies

- [Capability codes: `hub-cache`](https://github.com/mess-systems/naming-standard/blob/main/01-naming-conventions.md#43-capability-codes-unique-across-all-domains)

## Review trigger

A license or maintenance change upstream, or a better fit for the capability.
