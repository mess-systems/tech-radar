## What it is

Open-source small decision model for fast typed decisions in agents (Apache-2.0). Official site: [github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Fast routing and yes/no decisions before calling an LLM.

## Alternatives considered

LLM calls for every decision. Trial: limited scope.
