## What it is

Self-hostable team chat platform with an open-source core. Official site: [mattermost.com](https://mattermost.com/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

It is the example for the `chat` capability in the worked example (`chat.collab.corp`) and a gateway client (`svc-corp-collab-mattermost`).

## Alternatives considered

Other team chat platforms. Trial because the standard names it as an illustration.

## Where it applies

After it has carried the `chat` capability in a limited scope: promote to Adopt or move to Hold.
