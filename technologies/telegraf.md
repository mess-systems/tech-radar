## What it is

Open-source plugin-driven metrics agent (MIT). Official site: [influxdata.com/time-series-platform/telegraf](https://www.influxdata.com/time-series-platform/telegraf/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Metrics from devices and systems without native exporters.

## Alternatives considered

Prometheus exporters. Trial: limited scope.
