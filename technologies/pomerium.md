## What it is

Open-source identity-aware access proxy (Apache-2.0). Official site: [pomerium.com](https://www.pomerium.com/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Context-aware access to internal apps without a VPN.

## Alternatives considered

Authentik proxy, Teleport (Hold). Trial: limited scope first.

## Where it applies

- [Capability codes: `sso`](https://github.com/mess-systems/naming-standard/blob/main/01-naming-conventions.md#43-capability-codes-unique-across-all-domains)

## Review trigger

After it has carried the capability in a limited scope: promote to Adopt or move to Hold.
