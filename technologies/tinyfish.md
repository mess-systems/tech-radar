## What it is

Hosted web agent API: search, fetch, browse and act on websites. Official site: [tinyfish.ai](https://www.tinyfish.ai/).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Web automation for agents where sites have no API.

## Alternatives considered

Own browser agents. Chosen for reliability on live sites.

## Where it applies

- [Capability codes: `inference`](https://github.com/mess-systems/naming-standard/blob/main/01-naming-conventions.md#43-capability-codes-unique-across-all-domains)

## Review trigger

A license or maintenance change upstream, or a better fit for the capability.
