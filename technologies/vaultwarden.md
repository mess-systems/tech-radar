## What it is

Lightweight Bitwarden-compatible password manager server (AGPL-3.0), unofficial. Official site: [github.com/dani-garcia/vaultwarden](https://github.com/dani-garcia/vaultwarden).

## Why mess.systems uses it

Considered for the `passwords` capability for people.

## Alternatives considered

Bitwarden self-hosted, Passbolt. Assess: unofficial server, check support and audit needs first.
